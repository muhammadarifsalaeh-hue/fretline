# Córdoba logo source research

Accessed: 2026-10-23 UTC. Research only; no logo downloaded or registered.

## Confirmed official identity

- Manufacturer: <https://cordobaguitars.com/>
- Official registered mark image, opened and visually checked:
  <https://yamahaguitargroup.com/imgs/logos/cordoba_logo_tmguidelines.jpg>
- Parent company's IP guidelines:
  <https://yamahaguitargroup.com/trademark-guidelines/>

The official image contains the actual stylized Córdoba guitar-brand wordmark.
It is not the Córdoba city/tourism/university mark. Availability of the file is
not a recorded permission grant.

## Reuse evidence and limits

The IP guidelines, updated September 9, 2025, distinguish ordinary-word
references from logos and design marks. They permit accurate, non-misleading
references to products subject to their listed conditions, and state that
third-party design-logo use requires a suitable agreement. Their attribution
requirements identify Yamaha Guitar Group as the trademark owner.

The official [Terms of Use](https://yamahaguitargroup.com/termsofuse/) reserve
website content rights and do not provide a general reposting license for a
third-party guitar-discovery site. No explicit site/publication permission was
established during this research. These statements report source conditions;
they are not an independent legal determination of all possible uses.

Targeted Wikimedia Commons and logo-source searches did not locate a matching
guitar-brand mark with an explicit PD-textlogo or Creative Commons declaration.
Numerous results concerned unrelated Córdoba cities, institutions or festivals
and were rejected. Do not synthesize a replacement or classify it as PD merely
because it is a stylized word.
