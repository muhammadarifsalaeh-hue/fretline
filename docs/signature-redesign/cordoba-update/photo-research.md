## Actual downloaded-image inspection

Both root-downloaded candidates were viewed locally. In the first photograph,
the real Córdoba wordmark is printed at the upper-left of a joint brand booth.
Classical nylon-string instruments with slotted headstocks and tie bridges hang
on the left wall underneath that sign. The central performer and right wall are
Guild; those should be excluded from a Córdoba-only card crop. Exact model
names of the left-wall instruments cannot be established from this photograph.

The middle upright non-cutaway classical guitar under the sign is a suitable
editorial subject. On the 3000×2000 original, a starting region is approximately
`x=420..820, y=460..990`; inspect the crop before finalizing it. A wider left-wall
detail around `x=0..1000, y=440..930` can avoid the performer, Guild display and
most equipment. These are framing suggestions, not specimen/model assertions.

The real printed wordmark can be photographically framed from the upper-left
sign. If implemented, its reuse basis is the licensed photograph and its original
letterforms, **not** a claimed separate official vector-asset license or a newly
invented logo. Record photographic cropping and CSS color/contrast treatment.

The second photograph centers a performer visibly playing a Guild instrument.
The Córdoba-branded small instrument at its bottom-right is a ukulele. It is not
a replacement Córdoba guitar photograph and is not recommended for this card.
