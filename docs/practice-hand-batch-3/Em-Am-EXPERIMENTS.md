# Bounded Em/Am experiments and accepted derivatives

Accepted geometric candidates: **Em7** and **E7**, with separate exact audit
reports. Neither is an approved teaching pose. They are not substitutes for Em
or Am, and their chord names/voicings were not altered to inflate the count.

Rejected attempts retained for transparency:

| Candidate | Actual result | Deployment |
|---|---|---|
| Am fixed-root ring fit | 370 combined crossing pairs | Excluded |
| Am joint root/digit fit | 393 combined crossing pairs | Excluded |
| Em joint root/digit fit | 444 combined crossing pairs | Excluded |
| Em wider rotated-palm bounded fit | 377 combined crossing pairs | Excluded |
| Am wider-root signed neighboring-surface prior | Self-crossings 0, but 210 actual guitar crossing pairs, 984 wood samples inside, high-e collision and missed contact | Excluded |

The signed nearest-surface prior was only an optimization heuristic. On this
nonconvex connected skin it can penalize innocent points facing a nearby
phalange's normal; that bounded run sacrificed the chord contacts. The exact
post-fit rejection correctly prevented it becoming an accepted runtime pose.
The unchanged acceptance criteria were not relaxed.

The remaining adjacent same-fret contacts need a better constrained palm
orientation and usable side-of-pad contact binding (and possibly local authored
corrective deformation). More iterations of the same unconstrained point fit
are not sufficient evidence of correctness. The original model, topology,
weights, chord data, guitar IDs and protected product pages were not modified.
